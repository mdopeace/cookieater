# Privacy Policy — Cookieater

Cookieater does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any data. It has no analytics, telemetry, accounts, or remote servers.

Cookieater reads and writes cookies in your browser only when you use the popup. Cookie data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Exported cookie files are saved wherever you choose on your device.

The extension requests the `cookies` permission to read and modify cookies for the site you are managing, and `<all_urls>` to determine the active tab's domain. It does not access page content.

If you have questions, contact the developer through the support link on the extension's store listing.
